# AI-Health-Friend
An AI enabled android app that can be used to detect common diseases from symptoms and can give some some advice on that disease.use just need to select symptoms and our app predicts possible disease.


Introducing "AI Health Friend," an innovative Android application designed with a user-centric approach,
transcending traditional health solutions. The primary focus of this application is early disease detection, utilizing
sophisticated machine learning algorithms such as Naïve Bayes, SVM, and Random Forest. These advanced
algorithms ensure accurate predictions, facilitating timely initiation of treatment.

Upon predicting a disease, the application doesn't stop at diagnosis. It goes further by furnishing users with
comprehensive information about the detected disease and offering practical precautions they can take.
Additionally, the application takes a proactive step by sending an email containing pertinent information about the
disease to the guardian of the patient. This feature is especially valuable in situations where immediate access to a
healthcare professional may be limited.

"AI Health Friend" addresses the critical need for healthcare accessibility in the absence of a doctor. It acts as a
reliable companion, empowering users to take charge of their health. Moreover, the application promotes ongoing
health awareness through the incorporation of a user-friendly health news feature. This feature ensures that users
stay informed about the latest developments in health and wellness, fostering a proactive and informed approach
to maintaining well-being.
